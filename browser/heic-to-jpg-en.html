Why iPhone photos may not open on a PC

HEIC is used for iPhone photos. A PC app without format support may fail to open an intact file. Keep the original and use photo diagnosis to check the format and readability.

Convert without installing an app or uploading photos

Select one HEIC photo in the tool below, choose JPG and save the result. Photos are processed in your browser. JPG does not support transparency and recompression may change the image.

Can I just rename the extension?

Renaming .heic to .jpg does not change the format. Convert the image instead. JPG and JPEG refer to the same format. If conversion fails, copy the file again from the original device. Diagnosis cannot repair a file or rule out every kind of partial damage.
